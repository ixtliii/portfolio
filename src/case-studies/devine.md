# Overview

A Bag That Blooms is a speculative launch for a LOEWE bag, made as a concept project during my studies in DEVINE (Digital Design & Development) at Howest. The idea in one line: **a bag that blooms**. It rises out of a garden of gloriosa, alliums and thorned vines onto a coiled concrete platform, and that moment becomes the hero of an imagined campaign landing page.

<p class="cs-tldr"><strong>At a glance -</strong> a solo 3D and motion piece: I modelled, lit and animated the whole scene in Blender, then placed the animation into desktop and mobile website hero mockups to show how it could live on a brand site.</p>

# Inspiration

The starting point was the Whisker bag and LOEWE's earlier work with flowers, greenery and immersive, almost theatrical spaces. I wanted a scene that felt grown rather than built: a dark, overgrown garden where the product appears like something in bloom.

I collected references of sculptural, slightly surreal florals, twisting stems and glossy 3D forms, then reduced them to a few rules for the scene: organic shapes against hard ones, deep reds against a near-black night, and a single warm light that finds the bag.

# Process

## Sketching the space

Before opening Blender I sketched the scene as a room-sized diorama: vines snaking across the walls, spiky flowers in the corners, grass on the floor and the bag at the centre. Blocking it out on paper first fixed the composition and the camera angle early.

## Building and animating in Blender

I built the garden in Blender: gloriosa lilies, wire-like alliums, thorned vines and a grassy hill, with the bag hovering above a coiled concrete platform. The animation is short and loops: the camera settles in, the garden comes alive and the bag lowers into place.

# On the website

To show how the piece could work in context, I placed the animation into a desktop and a mobile hero, the first thing a visitor would see when landing on the campaign.
